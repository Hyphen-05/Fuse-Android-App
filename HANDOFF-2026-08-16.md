## The thing most worth knowing

The calibration run overturned an earlier conclusion of mine. I had identified the cubic curve in
`ColorConverter.hsvToRgb` as the likely cause of the app being too dim, and was heading toward
removing it. **Measurement says that would have been wrong** — the strips are strongly compressive
(light ≈ byte^0.4), and the cube roughly compensates. This is now in CLAUDE.md's hardware section
and in `tools/calibration/README.md`. Do not act on the old reasoning if you find traces of it.

The same measurement says F5's dimness complaint was real *and* that round 2's fix probably
resolved it (delivered light went from ~10% to ~53%) — **Joe has never re-tested round 2**. It is
the cheapest open item in the project and needs nothing but him looking at a strip.
