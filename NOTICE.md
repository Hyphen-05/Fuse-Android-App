# Third-party content

## Tears of Steel

`app/src/main/res/raw/dark_a.mp4` and `dark_b.mp4` are four-second excerpts of **Tears of Steel**,
(CC) Blender Foundation, <https://mango.blender.org>, used under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>).

They are cut from the frames the Perception Lab's block 7 traces were computed from, by
`tools/ambiance-bench/cut.py`. The attribution is shown in the app, in block 7's briefing, because
CC-BY asks for it to reach whoever sees the clip — not only whoever reads the repository. Keep it
there if the block changes.

Nothing else in this repository is third-party content.
